# Hub Connect
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Web. A Next.js workspace for brand projects.

## Users
The primary user is a social media manager switching between brands. Each brand has its own voice, channels, media, and schedule.

## Product purpose
Carry a brief and a brand’s saved voice through caption editing, media approval, and a scheduled post that survives a restart.

## Constraints
Users plan posts for Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok in Hub Connect. Posts to a linked Facebook Page and its Instagram account publish automatically at the scheduled time; TikTok posts are published by the user. Caption generation uses Claude Sonnet 5.5 with the project's brand voice. Every scheduling decision shows the selected destinations and local time. A generation failure preserves the user's work.
